01 · Embeddings

Words become
numbers.

"How is the weather today" splits into five GPT-2 byte-pair tokens. Each id indexes a row of wte; each position indexes a row of wpe; the two are added elementwise. That sum is the residual stream — the tensor all twelve blocks read from and write back into.

Six stages,

05 · Training vs inference

						
One number tells you
how wrong you were.

						

							Inference samples from the distribution. Training does not: it reads off the
							probability the model gave the token that actually came next and takes its
							negative log. That single number is the loss, and lowering it is the whole of
							training.
						

						
Temperature is an inference-time knob. Changing it on 04 moves the loss shown here, which is exactly why it is never used during training.
