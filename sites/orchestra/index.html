01 · Four zones, one estimate

A council of agents,
not a single model.

Orchestra estimates a battery's State of Health and Remaining Useful Life by routing telemetry through four zones: input, a multi-agent council, uncertainty-aware aggregation, and a single unified output. Every number the council produces has already been peer-reviewed.

02 · What goes in

Telemetry becomes
a feature vector.

Cycle index, capacity, voltage and pack temperature are read every cycle and projected through a small transformer encoder into a 768-dimensional feature space before reaching the agent council.

Readouts update live, the same way sensor data streams in.
03 · Who does the estimating

Every estimate is
peer-reviewed.

SOH and RUL each have their own group of independent agents. A Risk module and an Uncertainty module sit alongside them, all feeding a shared consensus core. Click any orb or monolith for detail.

Agents work independently with complementary estimates.

06 · Why bother with a council

One bad sensor
shouldn't break the estimate.

A single estimator has no way to know when its own input has gone bad. A council can — the other members simply outvote it.
