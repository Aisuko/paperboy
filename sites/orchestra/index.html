01 · The orchestrator

Four agents argue.
One scheduler decides when to stop.

Orchestra holds a question, runs a council over it, moves each agent's belief to the others, and publishes only when they actually agree. It never answers anything itself — it schedules, routes and gates.

05 · What the guards are for

						
A debate that cannot stop
is not a scheduler.

						

							Four things go wrong without guards: the council echoes itself, one confident
							member hijacks it, everyone drifts as one body, or the channel stops carrying
							certainty at all. Each switch removes one and re-runs the debate.
						

						
Left wall keeps every guard · right wall loses one
