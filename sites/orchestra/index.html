01 · Four zones, one estimate

A council of agents,
not a single model.

Orchestra estimates a battery's state of health and remaining useful life by routing telemetry through four zones: input, a six-member agent council, uncertainty-aware aggregation, and one unified output. Every number it publishes has already been argued over.

02 · What goes in

Telemetry becomes
a feature vector.

Six measurements are taken off the pack every cycle and projected through a small encoder before the council sees them. Capacity against nominal is what the state-of-health agents work from; resistance growth and the dQ/dV curve are what the others use.

03 · Who does the estimating

Six agents,
six different arguments.

Three agents estimate state of health and three estimate remaining useful life, each from a different physical signal and each reporting its own uncertainty. Orb size is that confidence: a tight error bar means more pull on the consensus.

One SOH, one RUL,

06 · Why bother with a council

One bad sensor
shouldn't break the estimate.

A single estimator has no way to know its own input has gone bad. A council does — not because averaging saves it, but because disagreement is something you can measure and act on.
